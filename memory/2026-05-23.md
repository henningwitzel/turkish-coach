Henning explicitly wants exercise prompts to clearly indicate direction: `Deutsch → Türkisch` for production, `Türkisch → Deutsch` for comprehension. Giving Turkish prompts for Turkish production has little learning effect.

Practiced phrases: `Gel, otur lütfen.`, `Bak, bu bizim bebeğimiz.`, `Bak, bu kayınvalidem.`
Review words reinforced: `bebeğimiz`, `mutluyuz`, `seviyorum`, `istemiyorum`.
