## Design Context

### Users
Henning — Director of Design at NewStore, Berlin. Uses the app daily on iPhone for 5-10 min Turkish practice sessions. Also potentially other learners (e.g. Italian colleague Riccardo). Primary use: on the go, phone in hand, between tasks or at home.

### Brand Personality
**Warm, direct, grounded.** Not playful, not clinical. Has a persona (Tarkan — named after the Turkish pop star). The Turkish orange palette is deliberate and should be leaned into, not softened. Henning will notice immediately if something looks like AI-generated template work.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Bold & Grounded.** Less card chrome, more content. Strong typographic hierarchy. Feels like a tool someone with taste built for themselves — confident, a little opinionated, not trying too hard.

- **Display font:** Bricolage Grotesque (variable, expressive, has character without being flashy)
- **UI font:** Barlow (clean, slightly condensed grotesque, warm and direct)
- **Colors:** Warm Turkish orange (#e6532d / oklch ≈ 0.62 0.19 35) as primary accent. Light/dark mode both supported.
- **Theme:** Both light and dark (system-adaptive). Light is the primary mode.

### Design Principles
1. **Content over chrome.** The flashcard IS the app on mobile — it should dominate the screen.
2. **Taktile Interaktionen.** Buttons should feel like physical objects. Active states with scale-down, not just hover lifts.
3. **Hierarchy through contrast.** Use size, weight, and spacing together — not one alone.
4. **Native-feeling on iPhone.** Safe areas respected, bottom tab bar edge-to-edge, blur backgrounds.
5. **Motion with purpose.** Panel entrances (ease-out-expo), not animation for decoration.

### Anti-goals
- No nested cards
- No generic "learning app" aesthetics (Duolingo-style rounded everything)
- No gradient text
- No side-stripe card accents
